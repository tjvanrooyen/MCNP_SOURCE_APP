By binning emissions into a partition of the energy interval, the problem of too many DEP and DEE emissions and the resulting Monte Carlo sampling problem, is solved.

Use the energy partition in the file “EnergyBins_PE.dat” in the folder “data_EnergyBins” for both discrete energy photon (DEP) and discrete energy electron (DEE) emissions.

The LEB and UEB energies are specified.  The code must calculate the average energy of photon/electron emissions in every partition interval.  If NO photons/electrons are emitted in a given partition/bin, omit the emission line altogether.
