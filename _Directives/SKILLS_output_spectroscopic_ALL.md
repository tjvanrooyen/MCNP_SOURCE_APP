Write `ALL` SDEF outputs for all te radionuclides in the ICRP-107 RAD/ACK/BET databases into this folder.  The subfolder “ALL” shows example outputs generated in 2025 with the older code.  The new outputs will be better because photon and electron emissions all the way down to 1E5 MeV will now be included, which will open the door to modelling ACK electron transport inside cell nuclei.
